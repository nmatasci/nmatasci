## Hello
I'm Naim Matasci.

I build computational platforms that help research organizations turn data into discovery. My work spans AI-driven molecular design, computational pathology, genomics, bioinformatics, data engineering, and scientific software development. Over the course of my career, I have helped develop large-scale research cyberinfrastructure for both plant science and cancer research, combining expertise in computational biology, bioinformatics, data platforms, and applied AI.

Currently my work focuses on oncology. Previously, I worked on human evolution, biodiversity informatics and ecology, and plant sciences. 

This profile preserves some of the work I've done while at the [iPlant Collaborative](https://github.com/iPlantCollaborativeOpenSource) (later [CyVerse](https://github.com/CyVerse)) and the Center for Applied Molecular Medicine of USC ([CAMM](https://github.com/cammlab)). In 2021 the [Ellison Medical Institute](https://github.com/eitm-org) became an independent organization and my work moved to a separate profile ([@nmatasci-eitm](https://github.com/nmatasci-eitm)).

You can find out more about me at [matasci.info](https://matasci.info)
